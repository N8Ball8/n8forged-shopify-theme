# Security Policy

## Supported branch

Security fixes are applied to `development`, validated through a pull request, and released to `main`. Only the current production release is supported.

## Reporting a vulnerability

Do not disclose suspected vulnerabilities in public issues, discussions, pull requests, or storefront forms.

Use [GitHub private vulnerability reporting](https://github.com/N8Ball8/n8forged-shopify-theme/security/advisories/new) and include:

- the affected page, component, or integration;
- steps to reproduce the issue;
- the potential customer or business impact;
- any proof of concept that does not expose customer data.

Do not test against real customer accounts, orders, payment information, or personal data. N8Forged will acknowledge a complete report and coordinate remediation before public disclosure.
